    10FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Still wondering about something? Call or text 808-500-3222 or email privatesoccerhawaii@gmail.com.

    What ages do you train?

    We offer programs for children starting at 4 years old up to seniors aged 65. Sessions are age-appropriate, focusing on skill development, fitness and the love of the game.

    Do you work with beginners?

    Absolutely! We welcome players of all skill levels — from complete beginners to advanced athletes. Training is adjusted to fit each player's level and learning style.

    Do you offer private training and small groups?

    Yes, both. 1-on-1 private training is approximately 1 hour with a maximum of 2 players. Small groups of 3–4 players train for up to 1.5 hours, and groups of more than 4 players for up to 2 hours. You can mix and match — 1-on-1 one week, small group the next.

    Do you offer goalkeeper or speed & agility training?

    Yes. We offer 1-on-1 or semi-private specialized training for goalkeeping, speed and agility, and technical mastery. These are by appointment only.

    Where are sessions held?

    We train at multiple local fields. Exact locations and times are provided upon registration.

    How long are sessions?

    Session durations vary: children (ages 4–8) 45–120 minutes, youth (ages 9–17) 60–150 minutes, adults and seniors 60–75 minutes. We hold up to 6 training sessions a day, from 6 AM to 7 PM.

    What should players bring?

    Comfortable athletic clothing, soccer cleats and shin guards, a refillable water bottle, and a soccer ball if possible. We provide all additional training equipment.

    Do you offer camps?

    Yes. Seasonal camps help young players develop their skills, improve their game understanding and build friendships in a fun and safe environment. Current camps are listed in the Camps & clinics section.

    Can parents watch?

    Yes, parents are welcome to observe from the sidelines. We encourage family involvement.

    How do I register?

    Register online through the training request above or contact us directly — we're happy to help you find the right program for your age, experience and goals. Please sign the waiver before coming to your first training.

    Can I try a single session first?

    Yes! You can purchase just 1 training session to experience our training before committing. The introductory lesson is 1–2 hours depending on the number of players.

    Do you offer packages?

    Yes. Private or semi-private packages: 10 trainings at $61 per session, 20 trainings at $56 per session, 30 trainings at $49 per session. We also offer sibling discounts, multi-session discounts, early registration deals and split payments. For exact pricing and current promotions, please contact us directly.

    Can training be customized?

    Yes — every player is different. Sessions can zero in on a player's specific needs, such as weak foot, first touch, shooting technique or decision-making, and are adjusted in real time to be easier or more challenging.

    What happens if it rains?

    We notify families in advance of cancellations or changes due to weather.
